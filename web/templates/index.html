Nationwide deal scanner

Runs the same math as the calculator across every U.S. census tract's own median home value & rent, ranked by best cash-on-cash return for your actual down payment and loan size. This is a screening tool to prioritize where to dig deeper — not real listings, and not investment advice. Read this: price and rent come from two different groups of homes in the same tract (owners vs. renters), which can genuinely diverge — a mobile-home-park tract next to a normal apartment complex can show an oddly high implied yield that isn't a real, buyable combination. Treat surprising results as a lead to verify on the ground, not a confirmed deal. Every row is priced as a single unit, since a tract's median home value describes one home — use the Deal Calculator for real multi-unit numbers once you have a listing.
